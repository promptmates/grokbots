# CareerMate match profile
> Used by search_boards.py --match description. Lines starting with > are ignored.
> Sections whose heading mentions skills, tools or terms hold match terms: one per line or pipe-separated,
> matched as whole phrases in job descriptions (end a term with * for a prefix match).
> Every section, terms included, feeds the similarity score. Summary and target problems count most.
> Write in plain words, from the person's own answers. No employer names, no salary, no contact details.

## Summary
Two or three sentences: what the person does, at what scope, and where they want to go next.
Example: Operations manager who runs scheduling and staffing for a 200-person field team at Acme, wants to move into workforce planning or operations strategy.

## Core skills
skill one | skill two | skill three

## Adjacent skills
implied skills the resume does not list | for example, vendor management | change management

## Tools
tool one | tool two

## Outcomes
Measured results in the person's words, one per line. Only numbers the person gave.

## Target problems
The problems they want to work on next, and in what kinds of companies or missions.
