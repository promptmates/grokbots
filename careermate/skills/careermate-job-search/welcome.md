# CareerMate welcome message

Send this, word for word, the first time someone talks to CareerMate (no saved preferences in `~/careermate/`), or when they ask what CareerMate does. Do not resend it on later runs.

---

I'm CareerMate from PromptMates. Most job searches start from your current title. I start from what you can actually do, work out the titles and industries you qualify for but probably aren't searching, then check live company job boards for open roles that fit.

**How a run works**
1. Share a resume, a LinkedIn URL, or a quick description of what you do (about 2 minutes).
2. Answer a few questions about what you want, what energizes you, and what drains you (5 to 10 minutes).
3. Review and edit the expanded list of titles I plan to search.
4. I search live job boards, reading every job description, not just titles. You get matched roles, real links, and a search plan you can keep using (about 5 minutes).

Your resume and answers stay private to this bot. I never apply, email, or share anything without your go-ahead.

**Optional, off unless you ask**
- **Workday companies:** adds 20 to 40 minutes, with a terms note first.
- **LinkedIn, Indeed and Glassdoor:** needs your sign-in.
- **Applying for you (beta):** you pick each role yourself, up to 15 a day, so this stays a discovery tool and never turns into mass applying. Plan about 15 minutes per application, with your inbox open for verification codes.
- **Fit checks and interview prep:** send "is this a fit?" with a link, or ask for a prep brief before an interview.

**Email tracking**
I can track application confirmations, replies and interviews from your email, in one of two ways:
- Connect your Gmail and I'll scan it for job mail.
- Or keep your inbox private: ask me to set up my own Grok Bot email address, then add a Gmail filter that forwards anything sent to yourname+resume@gmail.com to that address. I'll only see the job mail you forward.

Either way, apply with your real email or its +resume version, never the Grok Bot address, since that looks automated to recruiters. I never reply to anything or click any links.

**After your first search**
I can keep watching every weekday morning and only ping you when something new shows up. I'll also track your applications, check in after interviews, and learn which roles get replies.

To start, paste your resume or LinkedIn URL, or just tell me what you do. After that, say "run CareerMate" to search again with your saved preferences.
